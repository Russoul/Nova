////////// NovaKernel.txt — the trusted kernel, rule by rule //////////

////////// Preface //////////

This file writes out every rule the kernel (Nova.Kernel)
implements: the fuel-bounded normalizer, proof-spine typing,
proof-term checking for equality, and item-level re-checking over
annotation skeletons. It exists so the kernel can be audited against
docs/NovaFoundation.txt clause by clause — every rule below is an
instance or a derivable composite of Foundation's rules, and each one
names its justification.

Position in the pipeline (docs/NovaPipeline.txt): everything upstream
— elaborator, discharge engine, AI — is untrusted and merely PROPOSES;
the kernel re-establishes each judgement from its own signature Σ and
is the only component whose verdict counts. Nothing here searches and
nothing here chooses: every rule is syntax-directed, every premise is
checked mechanically, and the sole inputs beyond the core term are the
proof terms and the skeleton the elaborator hands over.

Notation is Foundation's (contexts Γ, signature Σ, elements t, types
T, judgemental equality ≐, definitional contraction ≜), with three
kernel-only judgement forms added:

  nf(t) ⇓ t′ , nf(T) ⇓ T′        fuel-bounded normalization
  Σ; Γ ⊦ ᾱ ⇐ˢ Δ ⇝ ū              stated spines (§3): each entry a
                                 proof naming its element
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ▷ t₀ ≐ t₁ : T         proof checking — ONE channel; a
                                 type equation is the T = 𝕍 instance.
                                 Foundation's ≐ is derived notation
                                 for a ⋆-typing, and a proof term is
                                 precisely the evidence it is checked
                                 by
  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩              item-level checking (skeleton sk)
  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇒ T ⟨sk⟩              item-level inference
  Σ; Γ ⊦ T : 𝕍 ⟨sk⟩             item-level formation
  Σ; Γ ⊦ 𝒮 qsig ⟨sk⟩             item-level QIIT signature checking (§8)
  Σ; Γ ⊦ 𝔽 poly ⟨sk⟩             item-level polynomial checking (inside
                                 ν formation — Foundation's poly-* rules)

Every kernel judgement is decided inside a FUEL MONAD: a computation
either returns, fails with a reason, or exhausts its fuel — and fuel
exhaustion is REJECTION, so the kernel is total and every artifact
gets a verdict. Fuel is supplied per entry point by the producer's
margin (the elaborator knows its own step counts); one unit is spent
per ≜-contraction, nothing else costs fuel.

////////// 1. Normalization: nf, one fuel per contraction //////////

nf mirrors Foundation's ≜ rules clause for clause and normalizes
everywhere (under binders, in all components). The congruence clauses
are not listed; the contraction clauses, each of which burns one unit
of fuel, are exactly:

  (λ f) e            ≜  f[id, e]                    # el-pi-beta
  let a b            ≜  b[id, a, ⋆]                 # el-let-beta (a let is
                                                    # ALWAYS a redex: normal
                                                    # forms contain no let)
  (a , b) .π₁        ≜  a                           # el-sigma-beta₁
  (a , b) .π₂        ≜  b                           # el-sigma-beta₂
  ℕ-elim z s Z       ≜  z                           # el-nat-beta-z
  ℕ-elim z s (S n)   ≜  s[id, n, ℕ-elim z s n]      # el-nat-beta-s
  ⊎-elim l r (inj₁ a) ≜ l[id, a]                    # el-sum-beta₁
  ⊎-elim l r (inj₂ b) ≜ r[id, b]                    # el-sum-beta₂
  quot-elim f (class a) ≜ f[id, a]                  # el-quot-beta
  out (corec 𝔽 a f x) ≜ map_𝔽 hᵉˡ f[id, x]         # el-nu-beta; map_𝔽 and
                                                    # hᵉˡ = λ (corec 𝔽 a f[↑] ☐₀)
                                                    # expand by Foundation's
                                                    # ≜-clauses at contraction
                                                    # time (one fuel unit)
  𝒮.𝕤-elim m̄ ē (𝒮′.𝕔 θ) ≜ m_𝕔 θ⟨φᵉˡ⟩               # el-qiit-beta; fires
                                                    # only when 𝒮 and 𝒮′ are
                                                    # IDENTICAL after nf
  x[e˲]              ≜  a[e˲]      (Σ ∋ Γ ⊦ x ≔ a : A)   # el-sig-beta
  x[e˲]              ≜  T[e˲]      (Σ ∋ Γ ⊦ x ≔ T : 𝕍)  # el-sig-beta at 𝕍

The two el-sig-beta clauses (δ) belong to nf, the engine-facing
normalizer (kInferBare, typing a hole's value). NO VERDICT PATH runs
them: every rule below compares by the β-JOIN — nf without the δ
clauses, a definition reference a stuck head — and a δ-apart spelling
meets the position through a proof (x-δ, δ-all, §4) or a switch (§8).

(El is retired — a small code IS its type, by cumulativity
code-lift; there are no decoding clauses.)

Ω adds exactly TWO contraction clauses — the syntax-directed
instances of the admissible code-squash-idem (Prf is retired: a
prop's OWN head marks it, and an Ω-NEUTRAL under ∥·∥ stays stuck):

  ∥(l ≡ r ∈ A)∥      ≜  (l ≡ r ∈ A)   # code-squash-idem instances
  ∥∥A∥∥              ≜  ∥A∥           #   (squash is idempotent on props)

Otherwise ∥-∥ is inert: ∥A∥, as a type, does NOT reduce to A
(realizer irrelevance is the point of the squash), equality props
(l ≡ r ∈ A) — Ω-valued, per Foundation — have no contraction, and ⋆
has no eliminator. nf treats Ω/∥-∥/≡/⋆ congruently beyond the
clauses above.

QIIT formers (NovaFoundation.txt, QIIT section) normalize congruently
everywhere they embed Nova syntax — inside a carried signature's
external pieces, the eliminator's motives and methods, index and
constructor spines — so the signature-identity test above is plain
syntactic equality of normal forms (Foundation's structural identity,
nameless: no α). θ⟨φᵉˡ⟩ is Foundation's section spine at the
eliminator itself: the contraction materializes one recursive
𝒮.𝕤-elim call per inductive component of θ, and each later
contraction of those burns its own fuel. Path constructors add NO
contraction — their content is a judgemental equation (el-qiit-path),
which enters replay as a step license (§4). A signature is inert
syntax and is never itself a redex.

Scrutinees are normalized before the contraction test (call-by-value
on elimination positions), so a stuck scrutinee leaves a stuck
eliminator — nf never invents progress. A signature reference to a
name missing from Σ, or used at the wrong syntactic class (a term
definition in type position or vice versa), is rejected outright.

Σ may be OPEN (Foundation, DEFINITIONAL AND OPEN SIGNATURES): during
an elaboration run it carries the run's assumed equation holes
and declarations. A reference to a DECLARATION is STUCK — typed
by el-sig-decl (type entries included), no contraction — and nf leaves it as a
neutral head. Constraint entries are nameless and never referenced by
terms, so nf never sees them; they exist for the equational theory
(an equation hole read through el-reflect). Only nf and equality replay tolerate open
signatures: Nova.Compute (the uncertified evaluator) assumes a
definitional Σ and rejects open entries outright.

(HOLE INSTANTIATION is REMOVED: the kCheckSolution/kCheckTySolution
legality gate and the declaration-to-definition flip went with the
elaborator's hole machinery — see NovaElaboration's preface and
PerfNotes "The cost of a hole". Σ therefore never mutates in place:
a declaration stays a declaration for the run's lifetime.)

Fuel is a LIVENESS bound, not a semantic one: under inconsistent
hypotheses a well-formed term may have no normal form (see
NovaFoundation.txt, preface), and the fuel bound is what keeps the
kernel total in that world. Exhaustion never certifies anything.

////////// 2. Proof terms //////////

The certificate of an equation is a PROOF TERM (Prf): a term-shaped
tree whose leaves justify equations and whose inner nodes are the
congruence rules of the Foundation, one per former, with a proof in
every child position. The kernel CHECKS a proof against its goal: it
decomposes the goal's sides along the proof's nodes, computes each
child's type from the node (the type flowing down, the inferred type
of a neutral head, or a carried motive), and demands of each leaf the
equation at its position. No paths, no rewrite steps, no finals: what
used to be "rewrite the goal at a path" is a chain of congruence nodes
around the leaf, and the goal is never edited, only compared.
(docs/NovaKernelRewrite.txt: proofs are terms with proof leaves.)

The grammar (constructors in parentheses are the implementation's
names):

  Proofs are written IN THE CORE'S OWN SYNTAX: a congruence node is
  spelled as the former it is the congruence of, with proofs in the
  children's places, and a term-shaped proof reads like the term it
  is about. α, β, … range over proofs.

  π ::=                                            (Prf)
    -- leaves that STATE their equation (⇒, §4)
      [t]               SELF: a variable or a signature reference as
                        its own reflexivity, at its declared type;
                        also Z, (), 𝟘, 𝟙, ℕ at theirs   (PSelf)
    | [t : T]           CHECKED reflexivity: t checked at T (with a
                        skeleton), stating t ≐ t : T      (PChk)
    | ⟨α⟩               reflection: α states its equation at an
                        equality prop l ≡ r ∈ A — the licensed
                        equation l ≐ r : A              (PRefl)
    | qpath 𝕔 ᾱ         an imposed QIIT equation, its
                        spine STATED (§3)               (PPath)
    | x-δ ᾱ             x[ū] ≐ t[ū] for (Δ ⊦ x ≔ t : T),
                        ū stated by ᾱ (§3)              (PDelta)
    | (α : T′ by β)     ASCRIPTION: the stated equation of α at T′,
                        β ▷ A ≐ T′                       (PAt)
    -- structure
    | refl              the sides join under β          (PReflx)
    | α⁻¹                                               (PSym)
    | α ; β             transitivity, middle COMPUTED   (PTrans)
    | α ; [m] ; β       transitivity, middle STATED     (PTransAt)
    | (α ∷ T′ by β)     the equation's TYPE converted: β ▷ T ≐ T′,
                        α read at T′                    (PConv)
    | δ-all x̄           every addressable occurrence of the named
                        definitions unfolded at once   (PDeltaAll)
    -- type-directed leaves (⇐ only, §7)
    | irrel ⟨sk⟩ | η→(α) | η×(α, β) | quot-wit(α?) | quot-wit[w ⟨sk⟩]
    | inj(α) | propext[s ⟨sk⟩, t ⟨sk⟩] | prop-lift ⟨sk, sk′⟩ (α)
    -- (irrel and prop-lift carry the SKELETON of the type(s) whose
    -- prop-ness they assert: a neutral prop infers at Ω through it)
    -- congruences (§6), spelled as the formers; eliminators carry an
    -- OPTIONAL motive in braces
      𝟘-elim α   S α   ℕ-elim{M?} α β γ   λ α   α β   (α, β)
      α .π₁   α .π₂   inj₁ α   inj₂ α   ⊎-elim{M?} α β γ
      α → β   α × β   α ⊎ β   α ≡ β ∈ γ   α / β   x[ᾱ]
      class α   quot-elim{M?} α β   ∥α∥   𝒮.𝕤[ᾱ]   𝒮.𝕔[ᾱ]
      𝒮.𝕤-elim{C̄?} [ᾱ] [β̄] γ   out α   corec 𝔽 α β γ

  A TYPED NEUTRAL is a spine of elimination nodes over a self or
  checked leaf, ascribed wherever a definition hides the shape the
  next elimination needs:  ([a] : A × (A → A) by α) .π₂  types the
  head a.π₂ as A → A from a's declared type IsNatAlgebra A, opened by
  the recorded δ proof α. Such a proof STATES its equation (⇒), so a
  node above it takes the type it states instead of reading one off
  the side. A proof element — a lemma applied to arguments — is the
  same thing: [sym] [ℕ] [x] [y] [h], arguments that are spines as
  typed neutrals ascribed to the domain where spelled otherwise, any
  other argument as [t : A] checked at the domain.

Substitution acts on a proof as on a term (every element it carries
— proof elements, spines, stated middles, converted types, motives —
under the binders its nodes cross): a proof spelled in Γ is placed
under b binders as π[↑ᵇ].

Three READINGS of one proof (§7): ⇒ a leaf (with symmetry and
transitivity over it) states its equation and type; → given one
side, the proof produces the other — the reading transitivity uses to
find its middles; ⇐ given both sides, everything is verified — the
only reading the type-directed leaves have. Which readings a proof
supports is decided from its SHAPE before any check (synthP, dirP in
the implementation): the kernel never guesses a direction.

The engine WRITES proof terms as it searches (Nova.Elaboration.Proof,
its proof library, untrusted): a licence applied at a position
becomes its reflection leaf inside the congruence skeleton along the
path (typed as the nodes of §6 type their children — a conversion
where a shape is δ-hidden, a typed neutral for a scrutinee, a checked
leaf for an argument), a δ-round a δ-all leaf, a closing step by the
type's shape its type-directed leaf, the rewrites of one side a
right-nested chain ending in refl, the two sides' chains joined
through the closing leaf, an equation proved at an exposed type a
conv around the whole. There is no intermediate language: what the
search returns, what the item's skeleton carries, and what the kernel
checks is the proof term.

Child indexing, shared by the congruence nodes (§6), the engine's
paths and skeletons (§8) — binders crossed in parentheses:

  elements:  𝟘-elim t → 0            S t → 0
             ℕ-elim z s t → 0, 1(2), 2
             λ f → 0(1)              f e → 0, 1
             let a b → 0, 1(2)       # body under value + unfolding-equation binders
             (a , b) → 0, 1          t.π₁ / t.π₂ → 0
             inj₁ t / inj₂ t → 0     ⊎-elim l r t → 0(1), 1(1), 2
             a →ᶜ b → 0, 1(1)        a ×ᶜ b → 0, 1(1)
             a ⊎ᶜ b → 0, 1
             (l ≡ r ∈ T) → 0, 1, 2ᵗ  a /ᶜ r → 0, 1(2)
             x[e˲] → 0.. (left to right)
             class a → 0             quot-elim f q → 0(1), 1
             ∥T∥ → 0ᵗ
             𝒮.𝕤 ē (code) → 0.. (the index spine)
             𝒮.𝕔 θ → 0.. (the argument spine)
             𝒮.𝕤-elim m̄ ē w → 0..n-1 (the index spine), n (the eliminee)
                              (the methods m̄ are proof positions of
                               the congruence node, not path children)
             out t → 0               corec 𝔽 a f x → 0, 1(1), 2
             ν 𝔽 (code) → (none)
  types:     A → B → 0, 1(1)         A × B → 0, 1(1)
             A ⊎ B → 0, 1            A / r → 0, 1ᵉ(2)
             x[e˲] → 0..ᵉ
             𝒮.𝕤 ē → 0..ᵉ            ν 𝔽 → (none)
  (ᶜ marks universe codes; ᵉ marks descent into an element child, ᵗ into
   a type child. The quotient relation is an Ω-valued element child. A
   carried signature 𝒮, eliminator problem ℰ and polynomial 𝔽 have NO
   child positions — a congruence node carries them and they must
   match the sides' syntactically, approximation A3 (§9); only spines
   and eliminees have proof positions.)

////////// 3. Stated spines: Σ; Γ ⊦ ᾱ ⇐ˢ Δ ⇝ ū //////////

Nothing a proof term carries is typed by a checker of its own. A
proof ELEMENT — the lemma instantiation behind a reflection leaf —
arrives as a TYPED NEUTRAL (§2, §4): a proof term that states its
own type, read by the ⇒ rules of §4 and §7 with no unfolding. The
elements the former proof-spine checker (inferP/checkP) typed bare
— a δ or path leaf's spine — are STATED the same way: each is a proof α whose ⇒-reading is a
reflexivity u ≐ u : U, and the element it names is u, at U.

  Σ; Γ ⊦ αᵢ ⇒ uᵢ ≐ uᵢ : Uᵢ    Uᵢ agrees with Δᵢ[u₀, …, uᵢ₋₁]   (each i)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ ᾱ ⇐ˢ Δ ⇝ ū

"agrees" is §7's type agreement (tyAgree): β-join, cumulativity (a 𝕌
or Ω code at a 𝕍 position) and the identity of carried signatures
modulo their β-join (A3, §9) — never δ. The two sides must be
syntactically ONE element (the reading of a self leaf [t], a checked
leaf [t : T], an ascription, or an elimination spine over them; a
proof that states an equation between two spellings names no
element). Entry i's telescope type is instantiated by the preceding
entries (sub-norm-ext), so a dependent telescope is walked in order.

What this buys: the kernel unfolds nothing to type a spine. An entry
whose type a definition hides is ascribed by its producer, (α : T′ by
β) — as a proof element's Π is shown to the next application — and
an entry that is an intro form (a numeral, a constructor spine, a
code) is a checked leaf [t : T] with its skeleton, typed by the item
level's ⇐ (§8) rather than by a second checker: universe codes, sort
codes, constructor spines and eliminator chains inside a lemma
instantiation meet the one set of rules the item level has, with the
item level's payloads (an eliminator in argument position carries
its constant motive, A1, and reflexive coherences, A4).

let-expressions remain outside proof terms: a let is always a redex
(§1), so the elaborator emits proofs let-free; a lemma whose body
wants one is referenced through Σ, where the item level checks lets
directly (§8).

////////// 4. What a leaf licenses (⇒) //////////

A stating leaf licenses one equation, derived — never assumed — from
its licence. The base of every typed neutral is a SELF leaf, at the
declared type, no unfolding (a reference's spine checked against its
context, β-only):

  Γ‖ᵢ = T                    Σ ∋ (Δ ⊦ x ≔ a : A)   Σ; Γ ⊦ e˲ ⇐ Δ
  ------------------         -------------------------------------
  [☐ᵢ] ⇒ ☐ᵢ ≐ ☐ᵢ : T          [x[e˲]] ⇒ x[e˲] ≐ x[e˲] : A[e˲]

  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩           # the item-level checker (§8): an intro
  ------------------------    # form as a proof argument, at the
  [t : T] ⇒ t ≐ t : T         # domain, with its skeleton

Reflection (el-reflect read certificate-side): the proof is a stated
equation at an equality prop — the equality prop itself, Prf retired
— and the judgemental equation is extracted:

  α ⇒ p₀ ≐ p₁ : P    nf_β(P) ⇓ (l ≡ r ∈ A)
  ------------------------------------------
  ⟨α⟩ ⇒  l ≐ r : A

Equality is Ω-valued, so this is the ONE pathway — squashed spellings
normalize to the prop by code-squash-idem's instances. Any other type
is rejected. nf_β is the β-whnf: an equality hidden behind a
definition arrives ascribed, ⟨(α : l ≡ r ∈ A by β)⟩. Γ is the context
AT THE LEAF: the binders the congruence nodes above it crossed are in
scope (a proof spelled at the root arrives weakened, §2).

  (α : T′ by β) :   α ⇒ l ≐ r : A    β ▷ A ≐ T′ : 𝕍
                    ------------------------------------
                    (α : T′ by β) ⇒  l ≐ r : T′
  # equal types have equal PERs; the ASCRIPTION — how a stated
  # equation meets a position spelled otherwise (§7, the positional
  # check is β-only), how a spine's head shows the Π the next
  # application needs, how a proof's type shows its ≡

A PATH leaf  qpath 𝕔 ᾱ  cites an imposed equation of its carried
signature: the kernel demands 𝒮(𝕔) = 𝔄 ending in El (l ≡ r), reads
the spine ᾱ ⇐ˢ ⌊𝔄⌋ᵗ ⇝ θ (stated entrywise, §3; the signature's
embedded pieces normalized, as the sort's are), and the leaf states

  ⌊l⌋[θ] ≐ ⌊r⌋[θ] : ⌊El 𝕦⌋[θ]

— Foundation's el-qiit-path read certificate-side, exactly as
el-reflect is above. That the carried signature is the one at the
position follows from the positional type check (§7): the stated
type embeds 𝒮 syntactically.

A δ leaf  x-δ ᾱ  is x-δ for the definition (Δ ⊦ x ≔ t : T) ∈ Σ:

  Σ; Γ ⊦ ᾱ ⇐ˢ Δ ⇝ ē
  ------------------------------
  x-δ ᾱ ⇒  x[ē] ≐ t[ē] : T[ē]

Read LEFT TO RIGHT (the → reading at the side x[ē′]) it is applied by
literal replacement — ē′ ≐ ē by β-join, the side becomes t[ē′] — with
neither the spine reading nor the positional type check: the
occurrence is well-typed by the invariant and δ preserves its type.
Every other reading (a refold, a check against both sides) goes
through the rule above.

  π⁻¹   :  swaps the sides
  π₁ ; π₂ : when π₁ ⇒ a ≐ b : A and π₂ runs → from nf(b) to c at A:
           a ≐ c : A; symmetrically when π₂ states and π₁ runs ←.

δ-all x̄ states nothing: it is a function of the side it acts on (→,
left to right only; under ⇐ it unfolds the left side and compares).
It is what keeps a computation-heavy join linear: a numeral test
unfolds a recursive definition hundreds of times, and one occurrence
per leaf with a β-join after each would be quadratic on both sides.

LICENCE NORMALIZATION is not a separate mechanism any more. A
candidate the producer stored normalized — a hypothesis whose sides
it unfolded and computed against the store, so that k + Z ≡ k
applies at an occurrence of ℕ-elim … k Z — is licensed from its RAW
type by transitivity over the leaf:  (norm_l)⁻¹ ; ⟨h⟩ ; norm_r  with
norm_l, norm_r the δ-all/x-δ chains that turned the raw sides into the
stored ones. The proof STATES its equation (the leaf states, norm_r
runs → from its rhs, norm_l runs → from its lhs behind the ⁻¹), so it
sits at a position like any leaf; the goal is never un-β'd back to
the raw spelling (an inverted unfold has no position to land on once
the unfolded body has β-reduced away).

////////// 5. Injectivity //////////

There is NO injectivity node. A proof passes from an equation to a
component equation only the way the theory does: by a CONGRUENCE
whose β-reduct is the component. S-injectivity is the predecessor
congruence — Foundation notes it as derivable (an ℕ-elim retraction
of S) — and the engine derives it so: from a licence h for S x ≐ S y
it emits

  [λk. ℕ-elim Z (k ih. k) k : ℕ → ℕ] ⟨h⟩   ⇒  pred (S x) ≐ pred (S y) : ℕ

a spine node over a checked head, STATING its equation (§4); read at
the position x ≐ y, its stated sides join to the goal's by β (§7: a
stating node is also a leaf). The retraction is inlined as a checked
λ rather than referenced through Σ so that the join needs no δ.

Foundation's TYPE-CONSTRUCTOR injectivity rules (code-pi-inj,
code-sum-inj, code-quot-inj, QIIT code injectivity indexwise) have no
proof-term form: no proof in the corpus ever cited one, and the
selectors that used to spell them (with their instantiation elements)
are gone. A component equation of a code equation is therefore not
extracted (tests/nova/elaboration/elab-injectivity records this: the
sites end in obligations). Should a proof need one, the node to add is
the rule itself, its binder-crossing instantiation stated (§3) as the
selectors' were.

What does NOT hold, as before: point constructors of a QIIT are NOT
injective (equation constructors may merge them — a quotient's cls is
the canonical counterexample), and equations between equality props
have no injectivity (equality is Ω-valued and inherits Ω's
anti-structural discipline: code-prop-eq equates all true equations).
Injectivity is a TYPE/CODE phenomenon only, exactly as in Foundation.

////////// 6. Congruence nodes and the position's type //////////

A congruence node is one Foundation congruence rule (or a derived
instance: el-sub-cong through the former's binders). Read against a
goal it DECOMPOSES the side(s) — under → the one given side, under ⇐
both, which must have the node's head — and reads each child against
its part at the child's expected type, computed from the node:

  𝟘-elim[π]       child : 𝟘
  S[π]            child : ℕ
  ℕ-elim{M?}[πz; πs; πn]
                  no motive: CONSTANT-MOTIVE reading (A1, §9) —
                    z : E,  s : E[↑][↑] under Γ ▷ ℕ ▷ E[↑],  n : ℕ
                  motive M (checked Γ ▷ ℕ ⊦ M : 𝕍, and M[id, n] ≐ E):
                    z : M[id, Z],  s : M[↑², S ☐₁] under Γ ▷ ℕ ▷ M,
                    n : ℕ
  λ[π]            child : B under Γ ▷ A       when nf(E) ⇓ A → B
  app[πf; πa]     f : ⇒ᴺ(f),  a : A  when nf(⇒ᴺ(f)) ⇓ A → B
  pair[πu; πv]    u : A,  v : B[id, u]        when nf(E) ⇓ A × B
  π₁[π] / π₂[π]   child : ⇒ᴺ(t)
  inj₁[π]         child : A                   when nf(E) ⇓ A ⊎ B
  inj₂[π]         child : B                   when nf(E) ⇓ A ⊎ B
  ⊎-elim{M?}[πl; πr; πt]
                  t : ⇒ᴺ(t) ⇓ A ⊎ B;
                  no motive: l, r UNDETERMINED (under Γ ▷ A, Γ ▷ B)
                  motive M (checked over Γ ▷ A ⊎ B, M[id, t] ≐ E):
                    l : M[↑, inj₁ ☐₀] under Γ ▷ A, likewise r
  Π[πa; πb], Σ[πa; πb]
                  a : 𝕂,  b : 𝕂 under Γ ▷ A₁ — the RIGHT domain
                  (ty-pi-cong / ty-sigma-cong; under → left to right
                  that is the domain the domain proof produces)
  ⊎[πa; πb]       both : 𝕂
  ≡[πl; πr; πt]   l, r : T (the left side's ∈-type),  t : 𝕍
                  # code-eq-cong (admissible)
  quot[πa; πr]    a : 𝕂,  r : Ω under Γ ▷ A₁ ▷ A₁[↑]
  x[π̄]            child i : Δᵢ[e˲ prefix]  # the telescope entry
  class[π]        child : A                   when nf(E) ⇓ A / r
  quot-elim{M?}[πf; πq]
                  q : ⇒ᴺ(q) ⇓ A / R;  no motive: f UNDETERMINED
                  (under Γ ▷ A); motive M (over Γ ▷ A / R,
                  M[id, q] ≐ E): f : M[↑, class ☐₀]
  ∥π∥             child : 𝕍
  𝒮.𝕤[π̄], 𝒮.𝕔[π̄]  child i : entry i of the reflected telescope,
                  instantiated by the preceding children
  𝒮.𝕤-elim{C̄?}[π̄m; π̄; πw]
                  methods: no motives — UNDETERMINED; motives C̄
                  (C_𝕤[ē, w] ≐ E): m_𝕔 : 𝔄ᴰ⟨𝒮.𝕔 δ⟩ at C̄;
                  spine as above; w : 𝒮.𝕤 ē
  out[π]          child : ⇒ᴺ(t)
  corec 𝔽[πa; πf; πx]
                  a : 𝕌,  f UNDETERMINED (under Γ ▷ a),  x : a

  𝕂 is the components' classifier: 𝕍 when the node's own type is 𝕍
  (a type), 𝕌 otherwise (a code). E is the node's own type; ⇒ᴺ is
  neutral-spine inference (context variables, applications,
  projections, out, signature references, eliminator heads — the
  heads whose types are recoverable without annotations), read off
  the LEFT side's part, in the context at the node. Every "when
  nf(·) ⇓ …" above is the β-whnf: a shape a definition hides reaches
  the node only through a conversion — `conv` around the node for the
  type flowing down, `scrut T′ πᵀ [node]` for a scrutinee's inferred
  type (the node then reads its children from T′). ⇒ᴺ itself still
  exposes the heads ALONG a spine by δ (the type of h.π₁ needs h's
  declared type opened; a bare spine has no place for an ascription):
  the residue of neutral-spine typing (§9), shared with proof-spine
  typing; what ⇒ᴺ returns is the head's declared type, which a scrut
  node converts.

A child's expected type may be UNDETERMINED (—): harmless for a node
merely passed THROUGH (refl at a sibling compares, needing no type)
and consequential only at a stating leaf, where the POSITIONAL TYPE
CHECK (§7) then falls back on the leaf's own side: any type a
neutral inhabits is judgementally equal to its ⇒ᴺ-type — typing
inversion — so the leaf's stated type is compared with nf(⇒ᴺ(u)) for
u the side's part at the leaf (the NEUTRAL-SUBTERM rule). Since the
node carries the context, this holds under binders too: a variable
the crossed binders bind is resolved against them. The multi-typing
that makes the positional check load-bearing lives at INTRO forms
(a class spelling inhabits every quotient that relates its
representative), and ⇒ᴺ refuses intro heads — the historical
exploits stay dead.

The carried heads — a signature variable's name, a QIIT signature
and position, an eliminator's motives and methods, a polynomial —
must match the sides' syntactically (A3, §9).

Soundness note: the positional check is load-bearing. Equality is
type-relative in this theory (a class equation at one quotient says
nothing at another), and the two historical exploits
(docs/NovaPipeline.txt, "Why this shape") both die on exactly this
check or on proof-argument checking (§3). The invariant every
reading maintains: the side(s) a proof is read against are
well-typed at the node's type, so a node is a rule instance whose
typing premises hold — under ⇐ both sides (the goal's are; a
computed or stated middle is, by the equation just checked), under
→ the given side.

////////// 7. Proof checking: the three readings //////////

Element equations — Σ; Γ ⊦ π ▷ t₀ ≐ t₁ : T. Both sides are β-JOINED
(every computation rule, NO δ — a definition reference is stuck,
whatever its classifier), then the proof is read against them (⇐).
A type equation is the T = 𝕍 instance — one channel.

  refl at α-identical sides is accepted without normalizing (the
  normalizer is a function: same acceptance set, none of the work).

The ⇐ reading, given l, r (β-joined) and the position's type E
(possibly —):

  refl          nf(l) = nf(r)
  π⁻¹           π ⇐ (r, l)
  π₁ ; π₂       if π₁ runs →: m = nf(π₁ → l), π₂ ⇐ (m, r);
                else if π₂ runs ←: m = nf(π₂ ← r), π₁ ⇐ (l, m);
                else reject (no computable middle)
  π₁ ; [m] ; π₂ π₁ ⇐ (l, nf(m)), π₂ ⇐ (nf(m), r)   # el-trans; the
                middle is NOT checked at T — it is equal to l by the
                first premise, hence well-typed
  conv πᵀ T′ π  πᵀ ▷ E ≐ T′ at 𝕍 (E ≠ 𝕍), then π ⇐ (l, r) at T′
                # equal types have equal PERs (el-ty-coe)
  δ-all x̄       nf(unfold-all(l)) = nf(r)
  stating leaf  π ⇒ a ≐ b : A;  POSITIONAL TYPE CHECK  A ≐ E  (E
                undetermined: E := ⇒ᴺ(l), §6);  nf(a) = nf(l),
                nf(b) = nf(r)
  congruence    both sides decompose by the node (§6); children ⇐
                their parts — or, for a node that STATES (a spine over
                a typed head, §4) when the sides lack its shape, the
                stated sides meet the goal's under β at the position's
                type (a leaf reading; §5's derived injectivity)
  irrel ⟨sk⟩    nf(E) ⇓ 𝟙 or 𝟘, or E is a PROPOSITION — ≡-/∥·∥-
                headed, or a neutral that infers at Ω through the
                carried skeleton sk (kIsProp: the raw spelling first,
                since whnf can unfold a prop spine into a stuck
                eliminator; a bare sk reads an eliminator head at the
                constant motive Ω)  # el-one-prop / el-zero-prop /
                el-prf-prop (proof irrelevance)
  quot-wit π?   sides class a ≐ class b, nf(E) ⇓ A / r; by the shape
                of nf(r[id, a, b]) (an Ω code): ⇓ ∥𝟙∥ accepted;
                ⇓ (wl ≡ wr ∈ W) — π ▷ wl ≐ wr : W   # el-quot-eq with
                the witness RE-DERIVED from the relation's shape
  quot-wit-prf w σ
                sides class a ≐ class b, nf(E) ⇓ A / r; check
                Σ; Γ ⊦ w ⇐ r[id, a, b] with skeleton σ (§8)
                # el-quot-eq, faithful: an ARBITRARY Ω-valued relation
  inj π         nf(E) ⇓ A ⊎ B; l, r same-tag injections; π ▷ x ≐ y
                at the branch type   # congruence at el-sum-i₁/i₂,
                as a leaf so el-one-prop can close 𝟙 payloads beneath
  η→ π          nf(E) ⇓ A → B;  Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ π ▷ l[↑] ☐₀ ≐ r[↑] ☐₀ : B
  η× π₁ π₂      nf(E) ⇓ A × B;  π₁ ▷ l.π₁ ≐ r.π₁ : A,
                π₂ ▷ l.π₂ ≐ r.π₂ : B[id, l.π₁]      # el-sigma-eta
  propext f σ g σ′
                nf(E) ⇓ Ω, sides prop codes p, q; the two
                implications checked as typings, AS FUNCTIONS over Γ
                (Γ ⊦ f ⇐ p → q and symmetrically — prop-lift; the
                form a surface term can hand over)  # code-prop-eq
  prop-lift ⟨sk, sk′⟩ π
                E = 𝕍, both sides PROPOSITIONS (kIsProp through
                their skeletons — the lift's load-bearing side
                condition); π ▷ l ≐ r : Ω
                # prop-lift-eq

The → reading, given one side x, a direction (left to right, or
right to left), and E; it produces the other side, unjoined (the
consumer joins):

  refl          x
  π⁻¹           π in the opposite direction
  π₁ ; π₂       left to right: if both run →, π₂ → nf(π₁ → x); else
                if π₂ states b ≐ c : A (A ≐ E): π₁ ⇐ (x, nf(b)), c.
                Right to left symmetrically.
  π₁ ; [m] ; π₂ left to right: π₁ ⇐ (x, nf(m)), then π₂ → nf(m)
  conv πᵀ T′ π  πᵀ ▷ E ≐ T′, then π → x at T′
  δ-all x̄       left to right: unfold-all(x); right to left: reject
  x-δ ē         left to right at x = y[ē′]: y = x, nf(ē′) = nf(ē),
                result t[ē′] — no other check (§4)
  stating leaf  π ⇒ a ≐ b : A;  A ≐ E (positional check);
                nf(a) = nf(x), result b   (swapped right to left)
  congruence    x decomposes by the node; children → their parts;
                the node rebuilt from the results
  type-directed leaves: reject (they need both sides)

The ⇒ reading is §4 for the leaves; a TYPED NEUTRAL extends it to
the elimination nodes over them (the head β-whnf'd for its shape — a
hidden one arrives ascribed, §4):

  α ⇒ f₀ ≐ f₁ : F   nf_β(F) ⇓ A → B   β ⇒ a₀ ≐ a₁ : A′   A′ ≐ A (β)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  α β ⇒ f₀ a₀ ≐ f₁ a₁ : B[id, a₀]

  α ⇒ t₀ ≐ t₁ : F   nf_β(F) ⇓ A × B         α ⇒ t₀ ≐ t₁ : F   nf_β(F) ⇓ ν 𝔽
  ----------------------------------        -------------------------------
  α .π₁ ⇒ t₀.π₁ ≐ t₁.π₁ : A                 out α ⇒ out t₀ ≐ out t₁ : ⌊𝔽⌋(ν 𝔽)
  α .π₂ ⇒ t₀.π₂ ≐ t₁.π₂ : B[id, t₀.π₁]

  α ⇒ n₀ ≐ n₁ : ℕ
  -------------------------
  S α ⇒ S n₀ ≐ S n₁ : ℕ

A node states its equation only when its head is a typed neutral
(typedP: a self or checked leaf, an ascription, or such a node); a
node over a δ or reflection leaf is read by decomposition, as any
congruence. Under → and ⇐ an elimination node whose head child is
typed takes the head's STATED type for its children instead of
reading one off the side (§6).

POSITIONAL TYPE CHECK, A ≐ E: join-syntactic, or cumulativity (a
licence at 𝕌 at a position typed 𝕍 — code-lift-eq; a licence at Ω
there — prop-lift-eq, its side condition established by the
statement: a stated Ω equation's sides are props). Nothing else: a
stated equation whose type is spelled otherwise than its position's
(by unfoldings no leaf addresses — type indices, a hypothesis at the
quotient's definition) arrives CONVERTED, `π at E by πᵀ` (§4), the
conversion a δ chain the producer records.

Type equations — Σ; Γ ⊦ π ▷ T₀ ≐ T₁ (E = 𝕍): no conv is admissible
(circular); the type formers are congruence nodes like any other,
their components at the classifier 𝕍 (§6), and the extensional
components that cannot be reached by any leaf are closed by
prop-lift (a prop codomain equal only by propext, under Π[refl; …]).
A universe-code equation is already a type equation by cumulativity
(code-lift-eq) — no transport is involved.

////////// 8. The item level: skeletons //////////

The kernel re-checks whole items bidirectionally. Its input is the
core term plus a SKELETON — a tree positionally aligned with the term
(same child indexing as §2), each node carrying zero or more
payloads:

  payload ::=
      motive T ⟨sk⟩       (PMotive)   eliminator motive + its skeleton
    | intro-ty T ⟨sk⟩     (PIntroTy)  ascribed type of an intro form
                                      in inference position
    | switch π            (PSwitch)   inferred ≐ expected, at a
                                      checked non-intro term
    | refl-eq π           (PReflEq)   the equation behind a ⋆ at an
                                      equality prop (el-eq-i)
    | wd π                (PWD)       quot-elim well-definedness
    | expose T π          (PExpose)   head exposure at a checked intro
    | scrut T π           (PScrut)    head exposure of an ELIMINATION's
                                      scrutinee type (the function
                                      applied, the pair projected, the
                                      ⊎/quotient/ν scrutinee): the
                                      inferred type converted to T
    | squash-wit e ⟨sk⟩   (PSquashWit) the witness behind a checked
                                      ⋆ : ∥A∥ (el-squash-i)
    | squash-elim e ⟨ske⟩  (PSquashElim) el-squash-e-prf: scrutinee e
        b ⟨skb⟩ ⟨skq⟩                 (inhabiting ∥A∥), a body b
                                      proving q[↑] under the raw
                                      squashee A, and the GOAL's
                                      skeleton (its prop-ness)
    | qmotives C̄ ⟨sk̄⟩     (PQMotives) QIIT eliminator MOTIVES, one type
                                      per sort entry of the carried
                                      signature (the core eliminator
                                      carries only the methods)
    | qcoh π₁ … πₖ        (PQCoh)    QIIT eliminator coherences, one
                                      proof per equation entry
                                      of the carried signature

A payload is consumed when used (a node may carry several; order is
immaterial). Missing children default to empty nodes.

Checking  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩ :

  1. If sk carries switch π: infer Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇒ T′ ⟨sk∖switch⟩ and
     check π ▷ T′ ≐ T. (The conversion is CERTIFIED, never decided
     by the kernel.)
  2. Otherwise, if sk carries expose T′ π: check π ▷ T ≐ T′ and
     continue checking at T′. (No formation check of T′ precedes the
     replay, and none is needed: replay is EXTRINSIC — spelling
     surgery presupposing neither side — both sides entering as bare
     syntax, each step's license carrying its own components'
     typedness (the equality prop a proof element STATES presupposes
     the equation's sides). Read declaratively it is a CONDITIONAL:
     given Γ ⊦ T : 𝕍 — an invariant of this pass, every expected
     type being built from the item's checked type by nf and
     structural decomposition — a successful replay makes nf(T′) a
     well-formed type ≐ T. And nf(T′) is all the continuation
     consumes: step 3 normalizes before matching the head, so the
     intro checks against the exposed head and coercion —
     judgementally the identity — transports the result. Raw T′ is
     never established well-formed (≜-expansion does not reflect
     formation) and never needs to be: it is a REPRESENTATIVE, like
     every annotation. This is PExpose; its equation-level twin is
     the bridge, where the same conditional reading applies.)
  3. Then by the head of t:

     nf(T) ⇓ A → B    Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ f ⇐ B ⟨sk.0⟩
     -----------------------------------------          # el-pi-i
     Σ; Γ ⊦ λ f ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩

     Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇒ A ⟨sk.0⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ▷ (☐₀ ≡ a[↑] ∈ A[↑]) ⊦ b ⇐ T[↑ ∘ ↑] ⟨sk.1⟩
     --------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ let a b ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                            # el-let
     # T lives over Γ, so checking b at T[↑ ∘ ↑] is fully general,
     # not an approximation: under the unfolding hypothesis
     # (id, a, ⋆) ∘ (↑ ∘ ↑) ≐ id (el-reflect on ☐₀ plus el-prf-prop),
     # so any valid body type is ≐ T[↑ ∘ ↑]. The definiens is
     # INFERRED — an intro-form definiens carries intro-ty on sk.0,
     # the ascription route.

     nf(T) ⇓ A × B    Σ; Γ ⊦ u ⇐ A ⟨sk.0⟩   Σ; Γ ⊦ v ⇐ B[id,u] ⟨sk.1⟩
     ---------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ (u , v) ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                            # el-sigma-i

     nf(T) ⇓ (l ≡ r ∈ A)   sk carries refl-eq π   π ▷ l ≐ r : A
     ---------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ⋆ ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                       # el-eq-i over replay
     # disambiguated from the squash rules below by nf(T)'s prop head

  el-nu-coind rides ⋆ too — COINDUCTION at an equality prop over a
  ν-type, the payload carrying the invariant, the endpoint proof and
  the one-step closure, each with its skeleton (all three are checked
  terms, not replay certificates — the premises are prop
  inhabitations, exactly the item-level checker's job):

     nf(T) ⇓ (l ≡ r ∈ E)    nf(E) ⇓ ν 𝔽
     sk carries coind R ⟨skR⟩, p̂ ⟨skp⟩, q̂ ⟨skq⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ ν 𝔽 ▷ (ν 𝔽)[↑] ⊦ R ⇐ Ω ⟨skR⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ p̂ ⇐ R[id, l, r] ⟨skp⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ ν 𝔽 ▷ (ν 𝔽)[↑] ▷ R ⊦ q̂ ⇐ lift_𝔽(R) (out ☐₂) (out ☐₁) ⟨skq⟩
     -------------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ⋆ ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                       # el-nu-coind
     # lift_𝔽 is the RELATOR (Foundation, coinductive section) —
     # KERNEL-computed from 𝔽 and R, like out's result type; the
     # closure's type is the rule's shape, never elaborator-supplied

     nf(T) ⇓ A ⊎ B    Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇐ A ⟨sk.0⟩
     -------------------------------------               # el-sum-i₁
     Σ; Γ ⊦ inj₁ a ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                (inj₂ analogously at B)

     nf(T) ⇓ A / r    Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇐ A ⟨sk.0⟩
     -------------------------------------               # el-quot-i
     Σ; Γ ⊦ class a ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩

     nf(T) ⇓ ν 𝔽    (the term's carried 𝔽 join-identical to nf(T)'s)
     Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇐ 𝕌 ⟨sk.0⟩    Σ; Γ ▷ a ⊦ f ⇐ ⌊𝔽⌋(a)[↑] ⟨sk.1⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ x ⇐ a ⟨sk.2⟩
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ corec 𝔽 a f x ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                      # el-nu-i

     nf(T) ⇓ 𝒮.𝕤 ē    𝒮(𝕔) = 𝔄 ending in El (𝕤 ī)
     Σ; Γ ⊦ θᵢ ⇐ (entry i of ⌊𝔄⌋ᵗ)[θ prefix] ⟨sk.i⟩ (entrywise)
     nf(⌊ī⌋[θ]) = nf(ē)    (T, the term's 𝒮 and both spines under the
                            β-join — a carrier spelled through a
                            definition arrives switched)
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ 𝒮.𝕔 θ ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                          # el-qiit-intro
     # the term's carried 𝒮 and nf(T)'s must be nf-IDENTICAL;
     # equal-but-different spellings go through expose/switch, as
     # everywhere. The term's 𝒮 is NOT re-checked here — it is nf(T)'s
     # signature, already validated where T was.

     nf(T) ⇓ ∥A∥    sk carries squash-wit e ⟨ske⟩   Σ; Γ ⊦ e ⇐ A ⟨ske⟩
     -------------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ⋆ ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                                  # el-squash-i

     T a PROPOSITION (kIsProp through ⟨skT⟩, on the RAW spelling —
     el-squash-e-prf's q : Ω premise)
     sk carries squash-elim e ⟨ske⟩ b ⟨skb⟩ ⟨skT⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ e ⇒ ∥A∥ ⟨ske⟩    Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ b ⇐ T[↑] ⟨skb⟩
     ----------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ⋆ ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩                            # el-squash-e-prf
     # disambiguated from el-squash-i above purely by which payload sk
     # carries; both erase to the same ⋆ (realizer irrelevance)

     Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ 𝟘 ⟨sk.0⟩
     ----------------------------                        # el-zero-e
     Σ; Γ ⊦ 𝟘-elim t ⇐ T ⟨sk⟩

     otherwise: infer Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇒ T′ ⟨sk⟩ and demand nf(T′) = nf(T)
     — a mismatch without a switch proof is rejection.

Inference  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇒ T ⟨sk⟩ :

  If sk carries intro-ty T ⟨skT⟩: check Σ; Γ ⊦ T : 𝕍 ⟨skT⟩, then
  Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ T ⟨sk∖intro-ty⟩, and return T. (This is the ascription
  route: how introduction forms sit in inference position.)

  Otherwise by the head — variables and signature references (el-var,
  el-sig-var; the reference's substitution checked entrywise against its
  telescope, each entry against sk's children), (), Z, S t, spines:

     Σ; Γ ⊦ f ⇒ Tf ⟨sk.0⟩   nf(Tf) ⇓ A → B   Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇐ A ⟨sk.1⟩
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ f a ⇒ B[id, a]                              # el-pi-e

     (projections analogously: el-sigma-e₁/₂)

  let infers when its body does (definiens inferred, as in checking):

     Σ; Γ ⊦ a ⇒ A ⟨sk.0⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ▷ (☐₀ ≡ a[↑] ∈ A[↑]) ⊦ b ⇒ B ⟨sk.1⟩
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ let a b ⇒ B[id, a, ⋆]                       # el-let

  ℕ-elim demands its motive — the REAL rule, no approximation here:

     sk carries motive M ⟨skM⟩       Σ; Γ ▷ ℕ ⊦ M : 𝕍 ⟨skM⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ z ⇐ M[id, Z] ⟨sk.0⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ ℕ ▷ M ⊦ s ⇐ M[(↑, S ☐₀) ∘ ↑] ⟨sk.1⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇐ ℕ ⟨sk.2⟩
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ℕ-elim z s t ⇒ M[id, t]                     # el-nat-e

  ⊎-elim demands its motive; the scrutinee's type is inferred (its
  head is neutral or an injection in the emitted fragment):

     sk carries motive M ⟨skM⟩
     Σ; Γ ⊦ t ⇒ Tt ⟨sk.2⟩    nf(Tt) ⇓ A ⊎ B
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊎ B ⊦ M : 𝕍 ⟨skM⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ l ⇐ M[↑, inj₁ ☐₀] ⟨sk.0⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ B ⊦ r ⇐ M[↑, inj₂ ☐₀] ⟨sk.1⟩
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ ⊎-elim l r t ⇒ M[id, t]                     # el-sum-e

  quot-elim demands motive AND well-definedness:

     sk carries motive M ⟨skM⟩ and wd π
     Σ; Γ ⊦ q ⇒ Tq ⟨sk.1⟩    nf(Tq) ⇓ A / r
     Σ; Γ ▷ A/r ⊦ M : 𝕍 ⟨skM⟩
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ f ⇐ M[↑, class ☐₀] ⟨sk.0⟩
     M a prop (kIsProp through ⟨skM⟩) ⟹ well-definedness holds OUTRIGHT
     (el-prf-prop — the ElimP rationale; the MOTIVE is tested, whose
     spine shape survives where a stuck instance's prop-ness is
     unreadable); otherwise:
     Σ; Γ ▷ A ▷ A[↑] ▷ r ⊦ π ▷ f[↑³, ☐₂] ≐ f[↑³, ☐₁] : M[↑³, class ☐₂]
     ------------------------------------------------------------
     Σ; Γ ⊦ quot-elim f q ⇒ M[id, q]                    # el-quot-e
  (the well-definedness hypothesis binds the relation instance
  directly — prop-lift)

  out is fully inference-driven — no motive, no skeleton payload

////////// 9. Acknowledged approximations and their scope //////////

The kernel is deliberately dumber than the theory; where it accepts
by an approximation, the approximation is an INSTANCE of a Foundation
rule (so soundness is unaffected) that may reject spellings the
theory would accept (incompleteness only). There are two confined to
EQUATION REPLAY (A1, A2), and three added by the QIIT extension
(A3–A5):

  A1  CONSTANT MOTIVE. In proof-argument checking (§3) an ℕ-elim
      argument is checked by the el-nat-e instance whose motive is
      T[↑], a ⊎-elim argument by the el-sum-e instance likewise, and
      a QIIT eliminator argument by the el-qiit-elim instance whose
      every sort motive is T weakened into the sort's context (the
      core carries no motives; in INFERENCE position a bare QIIT
      eliminator has no type at all — a spine references its Elim
      definition folded);
      a congruence node without a carried motive (§6) types the z/s
      positions of an ℕ-elim by the same reading (a ⊎-elim's and a
      quot-elim's case positions stay undetermined). A node MAY carry
      its motive, which lifts the limit at that node; the engine's
      translation emits none yet. The ITEM level (§8) carries real
      motives and has no such limit.

  A2  NEUTRAL SPINES ONLY (⇒ᴺ). Argument and scrutinee positions of
      a congruence node are typed only when the applied head is a
      variable, application chain, projection, out, signature
      reference or eliminator head. A leaf under, e.g., a bare
      ℕ-elim head is undetermined — the emitter's remedy is conv
      (§7), which re-types the whole equation at a spelling where
      the position IS determined. A δ leaf read left to right (§4)
      is exempt from both A1 and A2.

  A3  OPAQUE CARRIERS. No congruence node descends into a carried
      signature 𝒮, eliminator problem ℰ or polynomial 𝔽 — their
      embedded Nova pieces have no proof positions (§2); a node
      carrying one demands the sides' to match syntactically. The
      remedy for a signature-piece equation is a lemma respelling
      the whole former, via conv or expose.

  A4  β-ONLY COHERENCES IN SPINES. A checked leaf [t : T] (§3)
      accepts 𝒮.𝕔 θ introductions, universe and sort codes, and
      eliminators in ARGUMENT position (A1's constant reading) — but
      its reconstructed skeleton carries reflexive coherences only,
      so a spine eliminator's coherences are verified by pure β
      alone.
      An eliminator whose coherences need real replay is referenced
      through Σ, where the item level carries the full apparatus
      (coherences as PQCoh proofs, §8).

  A5  NO η CERTIFICATE FOR QIITs. el-qiit-eta (like el-nat-eta,
      el-sum-eta and el-quot-eta before it) has no replay final; for
      ν the gap is CLOSED, by el-nu-coind as a ⋆-payload (§8) — the
      admissible relational form, not el-nu-eta itself (whose
      candidate h would be a carried higher-order payload; the
      relational form subsumes it via graph invariants);
      uniqueness arguments live in the elaborator as lemmas, not in
      kernel proof terms (an el-sum-eta instance is proven as an
      ordinary ⊎-elim lemma at an equality motive, β closing both
      cases). The Π/Σ η finals are unaffected.

  A6  FIRST-ORDER SIGNATURE FRAGMENT. The signature checker (§8)
      covers the fragment the elaborator emits: no equation-code
      binders, no external λ (infinitary recursive arguments). Sort
      entries MAY carry inductive index binders (induction-induction
      — Con/Ty-style signatures check; the entry walk rebases each
      index code from its declaration site into the walk state of
      its use site). Foundation covers the rest; the restriction is
      checking incompleteness only.

NO δ FOR EXPOSURE (docs/NovaKernelRewrite.txt, CONVENTIONS): the
kernel's weak-head normalizer is β-only. Every shape a definition
hides — the Π of a checked λ, the Σ of a projected pair, the quotient
of a class, the ≡ behind a proof's type, the type a stated equation
sits at — reaches the kernel through a recorded conversion: the
expose/scrut payloads at the item level (§8), conv/scrut nodes and
the ascribed leaves inside a proof (§2, §4, §7). The producer records
them from its own exposure (the engine's whitelist governs matching,
not shapes: a value the elaborator never elaborated — a solved hole,
a synthesized type — has its skeleton RECONSTRUCTED with the
whitelist open). Proof elements and the neutrals a rewrite sits under
are TYPED NEUTRALS (§2, §4, §7): spines of elimination nodes over self
leaves, ascribed where a definition hides a shape — so proof-spine
typing is β-only, and so is every element a proof carries (a δ or
path leaf's spine: STATED, §3) and
every prop-ness test (kIsProp infers through a carried skeleton).
NO kernel-initiated δ remains on a verdict path. ⇒ᴺ at an
undetermined position (§6) opens a bare spine's head type by the
β-whnf, and a typed head is read from its stated type; el-qiit-intro
(§8) joins the expected type, the term's carried signature and the
index spines under β; two sorts at one position and spine agree when
their carried signatures JOIN alike (tyAgree, §7 — the structural
identity el-qiit-beta fires on; A3: a carrier is inert syntax, never
a proof position); a path leaf's carried signature (§4) and a corec's
polynomial against its ν-type's (§8) are joined the same way. The
last four were made β-only on 2026-09-24 after a corpus-wide
measurement showed their δ never deciding a verdict (8 spelling
changes, all in Qiit/cross.nova, both carriers unfolding alike). The
δ clauses of nf (§1) serve one engine-facing service, kInferBare
(typing a hole's value), which runs the LIBERAL whnf — never a
verdict path; the kernel state carries the flag explicitly
(KSt.liberal), so a verdict path cannot inherit it by accident.

The item level's checking fallthrough (a non-intro term with no
switch payload) compares inferred and expected by β and cumulativity
ONLY: every position whose spelling is δ-apart carries a switch proof
— from elaboration, from a reconstructed skeleton (a solved hole, a
synthesized type, an inline definition's Π-closure), or from the
data-item emission (a constructor's or eliminator's binder declared at
an external domain as written, meeting the entry type every kernel
rule reflects from the carried signature AS WRITTEN, modulo β).

Two structural caveats restated from docs/NovaPipeline.txt: an
annotation (motive, ascription, exposure target) is a REPRESENTATIVE,
never a canonical type — no consumer compares annotations
syntactically, only up to certified conversion; and the kernel
invariant is one-directional — kernel accepts the annotated tree ⟹
the erasure is Foundation-derivable at the stated type. Rejection
claims nothing.

////////// 10. Derivations: the kernel on proof terms alone (DRAFT) //////////

STATUS. §2–§9 describe the kernel as implemented: an item is a core
term plus a SKELETON, a tree aligned with the term that carries what
β does not read, and equations are proof terms. This section drafts
the design that replaces the pair: the kernel's input is a proof
term ALONE, and the core term is its ERASURE. Nothing below is
implemented yet; when it is, §8 goes and §2, §6, §7, §9 are rewritten
to the grammar here. Decisions taken in the draft are marked DECIDED.

10.1 The observation

The proof-term language already synthesizes. The ⇒ reading (§7) of a
typed neutral such as [x] .π₂ y produces both sides and the type of
the equation it states, and an element's derivation IS such a proof:
one whose two sides coincide. The checked leaf [t : T] ⟨sk⟩ is the
one place where a bare term and a skeleton still enter together, and
every awkwardness of the pair lives there or in the item level that
mirrors it — child indexing by position, "bad path", motives whose
binder depth the skeleton does not record, a substitution that cannot
enter skeletons, skeleton reconstruction for values the elaborator
never elaborated. Give the intro forms and the eliminators their own
nodes, carrying what β does not read, and the term becomes an OUTPUT:
the erasure |π| of the derivation. A substitution node π[σ] then
synthesizes |π|[σ] : A[σ] by computing, and there is nothing to
compare it with.

One language, then: a DERIVATION is a proof term, a proof term is a
derivation, read BIDIRECTIONALLY:

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ l ≐ r : T        inference: the type is an output
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇐ T ⇒ l ≐ r        checking: the type is known

In BOTH modes the sides are synthesized from the derivation; the
mode only decides where the type comes from. An element derivation
is a π with l = r syntactically; we write Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ t : T for it.
Checking is the elaborator's usual mode, and in it an intro node
needs no annotation — the type flowing down supplies what the
annotation would (10.3); the annotated forms below are the
INFERENCE-mode forms, the successor of the skeleton's intro-ty
ascription.

10.2 Grammar

Spelled in the core's own syntax, as in §2: a node is the former it
derives, with derivations in the children's places; a subscript is an
ANNOTATION — what the former's computation rule does not read and its
typing rule needs. α, β, π, … range over derivations; a type
annotation is itself a derivation (of a type), never a bare term.

Binders are as in the core (a child under a binder is read in the

when it derives an eliminator it did not elaborate. A1 and A2 (§9)
are then not approximations of this kernel.

10.3 The two readings

Inference: every node reads its children (⇒ or ⇐, as its rule says),
obtaining lᵢ ≐ rᵢ : Tᵢ, checks the side conditions its rule imposes
on the Tᵢ and the annotations, and states F(l̄) ≐ F(r̄) : T, where T
is computed from the annotations and the LEFT sides (§6's
convention). Checking at T: an intro node reads its children at the
parts of nf(T) — the domain, the family's instance, the summand, the
carrier — and needs no annotation; any other node infers and its
type must AGREE with T, or arrives converted (π by β). Type agreement
(tyAgree: β-join, cumulativity at 𝕍 for 𝕌 and Ω codes, carrier
identity under β) is the only comparison; there is no checking of
bare terms. Written for element derivations (l = r), the rules are
the rules of §8 with the skeleton payloads read off the annotations
or the type; a few, to fix the shape:

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π_A ⇒ A : 𝕍      Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ π ⇒ b : B
  --------------------------------------------------          # el-pi-i ⇒
  Σ; Γ ⊦ λ_{π_A} π ⇒ λ b : Π A B

  nf(T) ⇓ Π A B      Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ π ⇐ B ⇒ b
  --------------------------------------------------          # el-pi-i ⇐
  Σ; Γ ⊦ λ π ⇐ T ⇒ λ b

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ f : F    nf(F) ⇓ Π A B    Σ; Γ ⊦ α ⇒ a : A′    A′ agrees A
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π α ⇒ f a : B[id, a]                                # el-pi-e

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π_u ⇒ u : A    Σ; Γ ▷ A ⊦ π_B ⇒ B : 𝕍    Σ; Γ ⊦ π_v ⇒ v : B′
  B′ agrees B[id, u]
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ (π_u, π_v)_{π_B} ⇒ (u, v) : Σ A B                   # el-sigma-i

  Σ; Γ ▷ ℕ ⊦ π_M ⇒ M : 𝕍    Σ; Γ ⊦ π_z ⇒ z : Z′    Z′ agrees M[id, Z]
  Σ; Γ ▷ ℕ ▷ M ⊦ π_s ⇒ s : S′    S′ agrees M[(↑, S ☐₀) ∘ ↑]
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π_n ⇒ n : N′    N′ agrees ℕ
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ ℕ-elim_{π_M} π_z π_s π_n ⇒ ℕ-elim z s n : M[id, n]   # el-nat-e

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π_P ⇒ (l ≡ r ∈ A) : Ω      Σ; Γ ⊦ π ▷ l ≐ r : A
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ ⋆_{π_P} by π ⇒ ⋆ : (l ≡ r ∈ A)                     # el-eq-i ⇒
  # the type is an OUTPUT of the witness node: what π_P derives;
  # under checking, nf(T) ⇓ (l ≡ r ∈ A) supplies it: ⋆ by π ⇐ T

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ t : T′    Σ; Γ ⊦ π_T ⇒ T : 𝕍    Σ; Γ ⊦ β ▷ T′ ≐ T : 𝕍
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ∷ π_T by β ⇒ t : T                               # conversion ⇒

  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ t : T′         Σ; Γ ⊦ β ▷ T′ ≐ T : 𝕍
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π by β ⇐ T ⇒ t                                     # conversion ⇐

quot-elim: with the motive M and the relation instance read off the
scrutinee's derived type (nf(Tq) ⇓ A / r), the well-definedness proof
wd ▷ f[↑³, ☐₂] ≐ f[↑³, ☐₁] : M[↑³, class ☐₂] under Γ ▷ A ▷ A[↑] ▷ r is
demanded unless M derives at Ω (kIsProp becomes: the motive's own
derivation, no skeleton to thread). squash-elim, coind and the QIIT
eliminator follow §8 likewise, each payload an annotation. A
reference's spine x[π̄] is §3's stated spine, each entry a derivation
read ⇒ against the telescope; a δ leaf's and a path leaf's spines the
same.

For nodes whose children state PROPER equations (lᵢ ≠ rᵢ) the reading
is the congruence rule (§6): the result is F(l̄) ≐ F(r̄), the type
computed as above from the left sides. This is what makes one grammar
serve both: a congruence proof is a derivation with equations at the
leaves.

10.4 The substitution node                                    DECIDED

  σ ::= ↑ᵈ ▷ (π₁ : π_T₁) … (πₖ : π_Tₖ)      d ≥ 0, k ≥ 0

  Γ = Δ↓d ▷ T₁ … Tₖ  where  Δ↓d ⊦ π_T₁ ⇒ T₁ : 𝕍,
                            Δ↓d ▷ T₁ … Tᵢ₋₁ ⊦ π_Tᵢ ⇒ Tᵢ : 𝕍
  Σ; Δ ⊦ πᵢ ⇒ eᵢ : Eᵢ    Eᵢ agrees Tᵢ[↑ᵈ, e₁, …, eᵢ₋₁]      (each i)
  Σ; Γ ⊦ π ⇒ l ≐ r : A
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Σ; Δ ⊦ π[σ] ⇒ l[σ] ≐ r[σ] : A[σ]                            # subst

Δ↓d is Δ without its d newest entries. This is the substitution
lemma, admissible in the theory, made a rule of the kernel — as it is
in a CwF, where substitution is primitive. Its shape is a weakening
followed by a typed extension, which is the ONLY shape of
substitution the engine performs (a match's instantiation under the
binders it crossed, a hypothesis moved under a binder, a lemma
instantiated at its parameters); the telescope is carried because it
cannot be recovered from the entries' types (Tᵢ abstracts the
earlier entries, which the Δ-types Eᵢ have already substituted).
General substitutions — permutations, contractions — do not arise
and have no node. The node is INFERENCE-only: under checking at T the
kernel would need a Γ-type whose substitution is T, and σ is not
invertible. Harmless: what the engine substitutes are licences,
reflected typed neutrals, which infer; a checking-mode derivation
placed under a substitution is written in its inference form.

What it buys: a candidate stores its licence ONCE, a proof over its
own context, and is instantiated by wrapping (lic[σ]); a hypothesis
under three binders is ⟨☐ᵢ⟩[↑³]; there is no substitution on proof
terms other than this node (the current substPrf, which cannot enter
skeletons, goes), no rebuild of candidate sets under binders, and
sharing of a written derivation across its instances.

10.5 Sides given: the reading against erasures

Conversions are the one place a derivation meets sides it did not
produce: in π by β the proof β is read against the two erasures T′
and T — the third reading, Σ; Γ ⊦ β ▷ l ≐ r : T. For a proof that
SYNTHESIZES (a leaf, a node over synthesizing children) this is
checking at T followed by the β-join comparison of the synthesized
sides with the given ones — the `stated` reading of the current
kernel, made the rule. Only the proofs that synthesize nothing keep
§7's decomposition of given sides by shape: refl, δ-all, the
type-directed leaves, and the nodes over them, with transitivity's
directional run (→) inside. Every leaf under it is a derivation and
states its type, so the POSITIONAL CHECK always has a stated type to
compare, and — motives being mandatory — every position's expected
type is determined by the node above. The neutral-subterm rule (§6),
inferHead and the undetermined position disappear.

Erasure |π| is a function of the derivation (drop annotations, keep
the former; a leaf erases to its term; ⟨π⟩, x-δ, refl, … erase to
⋆-free equation content and never occur inside an element derivation
except as a witness node's proof, which erases to ⋆). Computation
stays on erasures: the join normalizes |π|, refl compares erasures,
δ unfolds a definition's stored erasure. Derivations are never
β-reduced.

10.6 Items, Σ, the pipeline

An item is a derivation of its type and a derivation of its body:
Σ; Δ ⊦ π_T ⇒ T : 𝕍 and Σ; Δ ⊦ π ⇒ t : T′ with T′ agreeing T. Σ stores
(Δ ⊦ x ≔ |π| : |π_T|): erasures, as now, since δ and β read them. The
elaborator's output is the derivation; the distiller prints erasures;
the round trip is surface → derivation → erasure → surface.

DECIDED: the engine's speculative search keeps comparing ERASURES
(β-joins, rewrites, matching are on terms), and typing enters only
where a proof is written: a proof's leaves are derivations. What the
proof library does today with elemToPrf/argPrf/chkSkel — stating a
neutral, deriving an intro-form argument at its domain, the constant
motive of an eliminator argument — becomes DERIVING the term against
its type (re-derivation), the successor of skeleton reconstruction
and of kInferBare; the same service re-derives a hole's solution,
the one value the elaborator never elaborated (unification produces
terms, not derivations).

10.7 What disappears, what remains

Gone: Skel, Payload, the child-indexing table of §2, the checked leaf
[t : T] ⟨sk⟩, the self leaf's bare term, checking-mode inference of
bare terms, inferHead, the neutral-subterm rule, A1, A2, substPrf,
skeleton reconstruction, the item-level fallthrough. Remaining kernel
comparison: tyAgree (β-join, cumulativity, carrier identity) and the
⇐ reading of conversion proofs against erasures. Remaining
approximations: A3 (opaque carriers), A4 (β-only coherences in
re-derived eliminator arguments — the engine writes refl coherences),
A5 (no η certificate for QIITs).

10.8 Migration order

  1. this grammar and its erasure as data (Nova.Kernel), beside Prf;
  2. the ⇒ reading of every node in the kernel, sharing kEqElem's
     leaves and the ⇐ reading; the item entry point on derivations;
  3. the elaborator returns derivations (every (Elem, Skel) site), the
     data-item emitters build them, re-derivation replaces reSkel;
  4. the engine: leaves as derivations, candidates as proofs over
     their context instantiated by [σ], extendCS a wrap;
  5. delete Skel, Payload, PChk, PSelf's bare form, substPrf; rewrite
     §2, §6, §7, §8, §9 from this section.

Open at the time of writing: whether the annotation of an intro node
should be the FULL type (λ_{Π A B}) rather than the domain — the
domain suffices for synthesis and is what the elaborator has; and
whether a proof-level let (α ≔ π in π′) is wanted for sharing beyond
what [σ] gives — deferred to the let-sharing item.
